Privacy Policy

At Ninnice, we highly value user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Ninnice mobile application handles user data.

1. Data Collection

Ninnice does not collect any personal data. The app:

  • Does not request name, email, or phone number
  • Does not collect location data
  • Does not collect device identifiers or ad IDs
  • Does not share data with third parties

2. Children's Privacy

Ninnice is designed for children under 5 years old and complies with Google Play Families Policy. It does not knowingly collect any data from children.

3. Third-Party Services

Ninnice does not use any third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics tools.
