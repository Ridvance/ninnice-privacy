Ninnice Privacy Policy

At Ninnice, we highly value user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Ninnice mobile application handles user data.

1. Data Collection

Ninnice does not collect any personal data.

  • No name, email, or phone number is requested
  • No location data is collected
  • No device identifiers or ad IDs are used
  • No user tracking is performed
  • No data is shared with third parties

2. Children's Privacy

Ninnice is designed for children under 5 years old and complies with Google Play Families Policy.

The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

3. Third-Party Services

Ninnice:

  • Does not use ads
  • Does not use analytics tools
  • Does not share data with third parties

4. Data Security

Since no data is collected, there is no storage or transfer risk.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
